    What are the OPD consultation timings at Khenat Hospital?
    +
    Senior OPD Director Dr. Narendra Murlidhar Khenat conducts OPD consultations from 9:00 AM – 1:30 PM in the morning and 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM in the evening. Dr. Kabir Khenat handles minor surgical care & wound dressings from 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM and 6:00 PM – 9:30 PM.

    +
    Yes, Khenat Hospital & Prastuti Gruha provides 24/7 emergency response, labor delivery care, and acute wound trauma dressing round the clock in Karvenagar, Pune.
